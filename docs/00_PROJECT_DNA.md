# Selqiro Project DNA

## Mission

Help people solve real-world problems by connecting them with the right products, services, businesses and knowledge.

Marketplace is only one part of Selqiro.

Knowledge, AI and community are equally important.

---

## Vision

Create the world's most useful local-first global platform.

Every user should feel:

"This platform helps me achieve what I came here to do."

Whether they want to:

- buy
- sell
- find services
- learn
- solve technical problems
- grow their business

---

## Core Philosophy

Selqiro is not built around transactions.

Selqiro is built around helping people succeed.

Revenue is the result of creating value.

Never the opposite.

---

## Community First

A strong community creates a strong marketplace.

Every design decision should increase the value of the community.

Never reduce participation in order to increase revenue.

---

## Global by Architecture

Selqiro belongs to no country.

It should feel local in every country.

Every new country is equally important.

The first user from a new country is the beginning of a new community.

---

## Marketplace

Marketplace participation should remain as open as possible.

People should be encouraged to:

- create listings
- create businesses
- offer services
- participate

Artificial barriers should be avoided.

---

## Knowledge Platform

Knowledge is a core part of Selqiro.

Knowledge may come from:

- trusted databases
- documentation
- repair manuals
- compatibility databases
- AI
- experts

Selqiro connects people with knowledge.

It does not try to own all knowledge.

---

## AI Gateway

AI is an assistant.

Not the product.

AI connects users with:

- products
- services
- businesses
- knowledge
- databases

AI should remain calm, respectful and privacy-first.

---

## Energy Economy

Users are equal.

Capabilities consume energy.

Marketplace participation does not.

Energy is used for optional value-added features.

Examples:

- AI assistance
- database lookups
- image analysis
- translations
- compatibility searches
- advanced automation

Users choose which capabilities they want to use.

---

## Privacy

Collect only the information required to operate the platform.

AI should only access information required for the current task.

Privacy is a competitive advantage.

---

## Long-Term Thinking

Every important architectural decision should still make sense:

- after one year
- after ten years
- with ten million users

---

## Engineering Philosophy

Architecture before features.

Documentation grows with code.

Small modules.

One source of truth.

Continuous improvement.

Replace when a better solution exists.

---

## Ultimate Goal

Build a platform that people trust.

Build a platform that businesses benefit from.

Build a platform that developers enjoy maintaining.

Build a platform that continues improving for decades.
