# Commerce Architecture

## Status

Future architecture.

Not part of V2 Launch.

## Principle

Commerce-ready, not webshop-first.

Selqiro should not start as a webshop.

Selqiro starts as:

- marketplace
- nearby services
- discovery
- identity system
- messaging
- AI assistant
- Energy economy foundation

Commerce may be added later if businesses need it.

## Why not launch with webshop

A full webshop introduces:

- cart
- checkout
- payment
- taxes
- shipping
- stock management
- refunds
- returns
- consumer protection rules
- accounting complexity
- customer support complexity

This would make Launch too heavy.

## What must be ready

Even without webshop, architecture should support future commerce.

Important areas:

- Brand Space
- product listings
- payments
- invoices
- Energy ledger
- business identities
- accounting exports
- product catalog structure

## Future modules

Possible future modules:

- Cart
- Checkout
- Orders
- Inventory
- Shipping
- Returns
- Invoices
- B2B ordering
- External shop integration

## External integrations

Selqiro should not try to replace every existing e-commerce system.

Future option:

A business may connect:

- Shopify
- WooCommerce
- custom webshop
- ERP
- accounting software

Selqiro can become a discovery and commerce gateway rather than forcing every business into one built-in shop system.

## Final rule

Do not build commerce before there is real demand.

But do not design Brand Space in a way that blocks commerce later.
