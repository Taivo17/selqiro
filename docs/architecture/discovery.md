# Discovery Architecture

## Purpose

Discovery is how Selqiro helps users find useful things they did not explicitly search for.

Discovery is not advertising.

Discovery is not interruption.

Discovery is a calm way to help users notice relevant products, services, jobs, businesses, stories and future knowledge.

---

## Core principle

Selqiro does not sell attention.

Selqiro creates discovery opportunities.

The user should feel:

This is interesting.

Not:

This is an ad being pushed at me.

---

## Discovery vs advertising

Selqiro should avoid classic advertising patterns:

- noisy banners
- many sponsored results
- irrelevant promoted content
- hidden paid ranking
- interrupting popups

Instead, Selqiro should use:

- Today's Story
- Today's Discoveries
- contextual featured items
- nearby discovery
- curated daily surfaces
- clearly marked featured content

Discovery should invite curiosity.

Not demand attention.

---

## Today's Story

Today's Story is the most valuable discovery surface in Selqiro.

It appears as a calm, elegant, narrow top area.

It can feature:

- business
- product
- service
- job
- event
- travel offer
- campaign
- launch
- future opportunity type

Today's Story is not just an ad.

It is a daily story worth noticing.

---

## Today's Story placement

Today's Story should be visible at the top of the main experience.

It should be present on desktop and mobile.

It should not dominate the page visually, but it should feel prestigious.

It should be:

- narrow
- elegant
- clearly clickable
- calm
- not blinking
- not noisy

Clicking it opens a larger story view with more information.

---

## Country and worldwide story

There should be one primary Today's Story slot.

This slot can rotate between:

- user's country Today's Story
- worldwide Today's Story

Recommended default ratio:

- 60 percent country story
- 40 percent worldwide story

Reason:

Most users care primarily about their own country.

But worldwide discovery creates curiosity and global feeling.

One shared slot is better than two competing slots.

Attention is shared in time, not space.

---

## Story expansion

The top strip shows only essential information.

Example:

Today's Story
Meister Remont OÜ
Paide • Vehicle repair

When user clicks:

Open expanded story view.

Expanded story may show:

- image or video
- title
- description
- business/product/service/job
- call to action
- link to Brand Space
- link to product/service/job detail
- location
- validity period
- trust/moderation status when relevant

The user chooses whether to explore more.

---

## Story content rules

Today's Story must stay connected to Selqiro content.

Allowed examples:

- product launch
- business opening
- service campaign
- travel offer
- concert/event
- job highlight
- useful seasonal offer
- new technology
- local opportunity

Not allowed:

- political advertising
- protest campaigns
- ideological campaigns
- violent content
- adult content
- gambling-like promotions
- hate or divisive content
- misleading claims
- content unrelated to products, services, jobs, travel, entertainment, business or useful opportunity

Selqiro should remain calm, useful and neutral.

---

## Story moderation

Today's Story must be reviewed before publication.

Recommended moderation flow:

1. Business creates story reservation and content.
2. AI performs first review.
3. If green, story may be approved automatically or queued depending on final policy.
4. If yellow, human admin reviews.
5. If red, story is not published until corrected or rejected.

AI is not the final authority for sensitive decisions.

AI flags risk.

Human admin decides when needed.

---

## Story quality check

AI can also help improve story quality.

Possible checks:

- image quality
- text clarity
- category relevance
- misleading content
- missing call to action
- unsupported claims
- too aggressive tone

AI should help business create better content.

Not only reject content.

---

## Story reservation model

Today's Story is a reservation, not an auction.

The user should not need to guess bids.

The system shows current Energy requirement.

If the business accepts, it reserves the slot.

---

## Dynamic pricing

Today's Story price should depend on demand.

Do not make far-future bookings artificially cheap.

Reason:

A business could reserve many future days cheaply and block others.

Pricing should respond to demand and scarcity.

Possible principle:

- one slot per country per day
- one worldwide slot per day
- if many days are booked, Energy requirement increases
- if demand is low, Energy requirement can remain lower
- price is determined by system rules, not hidden bidding

Business sees a clear Energy requirement before reserving.

---

## Cancellation policy

Today's Story reservation should be treated as a serious business booking.

Recommended principle:

- short mistake window after booking may allow cancellation
- after that, booking is final or limited refund
- exact cancellation rules should be defined later

Reason:

Businesses plan campaigns.

Selqiro must avoid unstable booking behavior.

---

## Energy purchase during reservation

If a business does not have enough Energy, the reservation flow should not break.

Example:

Energy required:
900

Wallet balance:
620

Missing:
280

User sees:

Add Energy and continue.

After payment, user returns to the same reservation flow.

This is required for good UX.

---

## Today's Discoveries

Today's Discoveries are separate from Today's Story.

They can show:

- featured products near user
- featured services near user
- interesting jobs later
- useful knowledge later
- seasonal opportunities

This is a discovery area, not a replacement for search.

It should make the portal feel alive.

---

## Featured products and services

Featured products and services may appear in two places:

1. Their normal organic position.
2. Discovery areas such as Today's Discoveries.

They may have a visually distinct card style.

But they must not secretly replace organic ranking.

Organic search remains trustworthy.

---

## Contextual featured content

Featured content should be relevant to context.

If user searches auto repair, do not show restaurant promotion inside auto repair results.

If user is browsing restaurants, restaurant promotion may be relevant.

Rule:

Paid discovery must be contextual.

Irrelevant promotion harms trust.

---

## Organic ranking

Organic ranking should remain based on:

- relevance
- distance
- freshness
- listing quality
- service quality signals
- trust signals
- category match

Paid discovery should not secretly override organic ranking.

If content is paid or featured, it must be clear.

---

## Nearby discovery

Near You logic remains central.

Discovery should feel local.

Examples:

- Today's Discoveries near Paide
- Featured services near me
- Interesting listings nearby
- local business story

If content is global, it should be clearly worldwide.

---

## Worldwide discovery

Worldwide discovery is useful for:

- global products
- global brands
- innovation
- knowledge
- international opportunities
- worldwide Today's Story

Worldwide content should not replace local usefulness.

It should add curiosity.

---

## Daily freshness

Selqiro should feel alive every day.

The homepage can change through:

- Today's Story
- Today's Discoveries
- new local listings
- new services
- Daily Discovery later

Goal:

User feels there is a reason to return.

---

## Launch fallback

During early launch, there may be no paid featured content.

Do not show empty advertising areas.

Instead, Selqiro can curate:

- useful listing
- interesting service
- new business
- local opportunity
- quality content

Possible label:

Selqiro recommends today

or simply:

Today to discover

This keeps the portal alive before paid discovery starts.

---

## Daily Discovery

Daily Discovery is a future personal discovery layer.

It may show:

- one useful article
- one innovation
- one interesting listing
- one relevant service
- one industry update

AI may help select it.

AI should not write long commentary.

It may provide short summary and link.

Daily Discovery should save user time.

Not become another noisy news feed.

---

## AI and Discovery

AI should assist discovery quietly.

AI can help:

- choose relevant discoveries
- suggest related services
- surface useful nearby opportunities
- translate when requested
- summarize external links with attribution later

AI should not interrupt.

AI should not invent news or claims.

---

## Translation

Original content should be preserved.

AI translation is optional.

User should know when a translation is AI-generated.

For global stories and brand content:

- original language remains official
- business can provide official translations
- AI translation is user-requested or clearly labeled unofficial

Rule:

Original belongs to author.

AI helps the viewer understand.

---

## Reporting and moderation

Discovery content must support reporting.

If a user reports Today's Story or featured content, admin should see:

- content
- advertiser identity
- placement
- reservation date
- category
- moderation history
- AI moderation result
- user report reason

Discovery trust is important.

---

## Accounting and invoices

Discovery reservations may be business expenses.

Businesses need:

- Energy usage history
- invoice / receipt
- payment record
- reservation record
- downloadable documents
- future accounting export

Discovery spending must be connected to Energy ledger and billing entity.

---

## Analytics

Businesses need to understand discovery value.

Future analytics may include:

- impressions
- clicks
- profile visits
- messages
- saves
- location reach
- category reach
- story expansion opens

Launch analytics can be simple.

Architecture should support more later.

---

## What Discovery should feel like

Discovery should feel:

- interesting
- calm
- curated
- useful
- local
- trustworthy
- not noisy
- not manipulative

The user should think:

I wonder what is interesting today.

Not:

This site is full of ads.

---

## Final rule

Discovery is how Selqiro creates curiosity without breaking trust.

Selqiro should sell useful discovery opportunities, not intrusive advertising.
