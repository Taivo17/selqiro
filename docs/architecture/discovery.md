# Discovery Architecture

## Purpose

Discovery is how Selqiro helps users find useful things they did not explicitly search for.

Discovery is not advertising.

Discovery is not interruption.

Discovery is a calm way to help users notice relevant products, services, jobs, businesses, stories and future knowledge.

---

## Core principle

Selqiro does not sell attention.

Selqiro creates discovery opportunities.

The user should feel:

This is interesting.

Not:

This is an ad being pushed at me.

---

## Discovery vs advertising

Selqiro should avoid classic advertising patterns:

- noisy banners
- many sponsored results
- irrelevant promoted content
- hidden paid ranking
- interrupting popups

Instead, Selqiro should use:

- Today's Story
- Today's Discoveries
- contextual featured items
- nearby discovery
- curated daily surfaces
- clearly marked featured content

Discovery should invite curiosity.

Not demand attention.

---

## Today's Story

Today's Story is the most valuable discovery surface in Selqiro.

It appears as a calm, elegant, narrow top area.

It can feature:

- business
- product
- service
- job
- event
- travel offer
- campaign
- launch
- future opportunity type

Today's Story is not just an ad.

It is a daily story worth noticing.

---

## Today's Story placement

Today's Story should be visible at the top of the main experience.

It should be present on desktop and mobile.

It should not dominate the page visually, but it should feel prestigious.

It should be:

- narrow
- elegant
- clearly clickable
- calm
- not blinking
- not noisy

Clicking it opens a larger story view with more information.

---

## Country and worldwide story

There should be one primary Today's Story slot.

This slot can rotate between:

- user's country Today's Story
- worldwide Today's Story

Recommended default ratio:

- 60 percent country story
- 40 percent worldwide story

Reason:

Most users care primarily about their own country.

But worldwide discovery creates curiosity and global feeling.

One shared slot is better than two competing slots.

Attention is shared in time, not space.

---

## Story expansion

The top strip shows only essential information.

Example:

Today's Story
Meister Remont OÜ
Paide • Vehicle repair

When user clicks:

Open expanded story view.

Expanded story may show:

- image or video
- title
- description
- business/product/service/job
- call to action
- link to Brand Space
- link to product/service/job detail
- location
- validity period
- trust/moderation status when relevant

The user chooses whether to explore more.

---

## Story content rules

Today's Story must stay connected to Selqiro content.

Allowed examples:

- product launch
- business opening
- service campaign
- travel offer
- concert/event
- job highlight
- useful seasonal offer
- new technology
- local opportunity

Not allowed:

- political advertising
- protest campaigns
- ideological campaigns
- violent content
- adult content
- gambling-like promotions
- hate or divisive content
- misleading claims
- content unrelated to products, services, jobs, travel, entertainment, business or useful opportunity

Selqiro should remain calm, useful and neutral.

---

## Story moderation

Today's Story must be reviewed before publication.

Recommended moderation flow:

1. Business creates story reservation and content.
2. AI performs first review.
3. If green, story may be approved automatically or queued depending on final policy.
4. If yellow, human admin reviews.
5. If red, story is not published until corrected or rejected.

AI is not the final authority for sensitive decisions.

AI flags risk.

Human admin decides when needed.

---

## Story quality check

AI can also help improve story quality.

Possible checks:

- image quality
- text clarity
- category relevance
- misleading content
- missing call to action
- unsupported claims
- too aggressive tone

AI should help business create better content.

Not only reject content.

---

## Story reservation model

Today's Story is a reservation, not an auction.

The user should not need to guess bids.

The system shows current Energy requirement.

If the business accepts, it reserves the slot.

---

## Dynamic pricing

Today's Story price should depend on demand.

Do not make far-future bookings artificially cheap.

Reason:

A business could reserve many future days cheaply and block others.

Pricing should respond to demand and scarcity.

Possible principle:

- one slot per country per day
- one worldwide slot per day
- if many days are booked, Energy requirement increases
- if demand is low, Energy requirement can remain lower
- price is determined by system rules, not hidden bidding

Business sees a clear Energy requirement before reserving.

---

## Cancellation policy

Today's Story reservation should be treated as a serious business booking.

Recommended principle:

- short mistake window after booking may allow cancellation
- after that, booking is final or limited refund
- exact cancellation rules should be defined later

Reason:

Businesses plan campaigns.

Selqiro must avoid unstable booking behavior.

---

## Energy purchase during reservation

If a business does not have enough Energy, the reservation flow should not break.

Example:

Energy required:
900

Wallet balance:
620

Missing:
280

User sees:

Add Energy and continue.

After payment, user returns to the same reservation flow.

This is required for good UX.

---

## Today's Discoveries

Today's Discoveries are separate from Today's Story.

They can show:

- featured products near user
- featured services near user
- interesting jobs later
- useful knowledge later
- seasonal opportunities

This is a discovery area, not a replacement for search.

It should make the portal feel alive.

---

## Featured products and services

Featured products and services may appear in two places:

1. Their normal organic position.
2. Discovery areas such as Today's Discoveries.

They may have a visually distinct card style.

But they must not secretly replace organic ranking.

Organic search remains trustworthy.

---

## Contextual featured content

Featured content should be relevant to context.

If user searches auto repair, do not show restaurant promotion inside auto repair results.

If user is browsing restaurants, restaurant promotion may be relevant.

Rule:

Paid discovery must be contextual.

Irrelevant promotion harms trust.

---

## Organic ranking

Organic ranking should remain based on:

- relevance
- distance
- freshness
- listing quality
- service quality signals
- trust signals
- category match

Paid discovery should not secretly override organic ranking.

If content is paid or featured, it must be clear.

---

## Nearby discovery

Near You logic remains central.

Discovery should feel local.

Examples:

- Today's Discoveries near Paide
- Featured services near me
- Interesting listings nearby
- local business story

If content is global, it should be clearly worldwide.

---

## Worldwide discovery

Worldwide discovery is useful for:

- global products
- global brands
- innovation
- knowledge
- international opportunities
- worldwide Today's Story

Worldwide content should not replace local usefulness.

It should add curiosity.

---

## Daily freshness

Selqiro should feel alive every day.

The homepage can change through:

- Today's Story
- Today's Discoveries
- new local listings
- new services
- Daily Discovery later

Goal:

User feels there is a reason to return.

---

## Launch fallback

During early launch, there may be no paid featured content.

Do not show empty advertising areas.

Instead, Selqiro can curate:

- useful listing
- interesting service
- new business
- local opportunity
- quality content

Possible label:

Selqiro recommends today

or simply:

Today to discover

This keeps the portal alive before paid discovery starts.

---

## Daily Discovery

Daily Discovery is a future personal discovery layer.

It may show:

- one useful article
- one innovation
- one interesting listing
- one relevant service
- one industry update

AI may help select it.

AI should not write long commentary.

It may provide short summary and link.

Daily Discovery should save user time.

Not become another noisy news feed.

---

## AI and Discovery

AI should assist discovery quietly.

AI can help:

- choose relevant discoveries
- suggest related services
- surface useful nearby opportunities
- translate when requested
- summarize external links with attribution later

AI should not interrupt.

AI should not invent news or claims.

---

## Translation

Original content should be preserved.

AI translation is optional.

User should know when a translation is AI-generated.

For global stories and brand content:

- original language remains official
- business can provide official translations
- AI translation is user-requested or clearly labeled unofficial

Rule:

Original belongs to author.

AI helps the viewer understand.

---

## Reporting and moderation

Discovery content must support reporting.

If a user reports Today's Story or featured content, admin should see:

- content
- advertiser identity
- placement
- reservation date
- category
- moderation history
- AI moderation result
- user report reason

Discovery trust is important.

---

## Accounting and invoices

Discovery reservations may be business expenses.

Businesses need:

- Energy usage history
- invoice / receipt
- payment record
- reservation record
- downloadable documents
- future accounting export

Discovery spending must be connected to Energy ledger and billing entity.

---

## Analytics

Businesses need to understand discovery value.

Future analytics may include:

- impressions
- clicks
- profile visits
- messages
- saves
- location reach
- category reach
- story expansion opens

Launch analytics can be simple.

Architecture should support more later.

---

## What Discovery should feel like

Discovery should feel:

- interesting
- calm
- curated
- useful
- local
- trustworthy
- not noisy
- not manipulative

The user should think:

I wonder what is interesting today.

Not:

This site is full of ads.

---

## Final rule

Discovery is how Selqiro creates curiosity without breaking trust.

Selqiro should sell useful discovery opportunities, not intrusive advertising.

---

## Discovery on listing detail pages

Listing detail pages are important discovery surfaces.

When a user opens a listing, Selqiro has strong context about what the user is interested in.

This allows useful contextual discovery.

Examples:

- featured similar listings
- featured nearby services
- related business profiles
- future Knowledge content
- future Brand Space content

Rule:

Featured content on detail pages must be related to the listing context.

Paid discovery must not become random advertising.

If a user views a lawn mower, show relevant garden or repair services.

Do not show unrelated restaurant or generic ads.

This protects user trust and increases value for advertisers.

---

## Service highlighting duration

Service highlighting should support different duration levels.

Recommended pricing principle:

- 1 day highlight: highest cost per day
- 14 day highlight: medium cost per day
- 30 day highlight: lowest cost per day

Reason:

Longer highlighting should be more cost-effective for serious service providers.

This encourages stable business visibility instead of constant short-term manipulation.

Short-term highlighting remains available for urgent campaigns or temporary availability.

---

## Temporary service location

Temporary service location is a special type of quick update for service providers.

Example:

Tow truck provider normally operates in Järvamaa.

Temporary location:

Tow truck available in Tallinn, Gonsiori street area until 16:00.

This can make the service visible in Tallinn for relevant service searches during the active time window.

Temporary location must include:

- related service
- location
- valid until time
- short message
- optional contact action

---

## Temporary service location visibility

Temporary location visibility must depend on:

- location
- time
- service category
- relevance

Example:

A tow truck temporary location in Tallinn can appear for:

- tow truck search
- roadside assistance
- car-related services
- relevant vehicle-related contexts

It should not appear in unrelated searches such as furniture, restaurants or general marketplace browsing.

---

## Free and Energy-based temporary locations

Launch principle:

Each service provider may have one free temporary service location per day.

Additional temporary service locations can require Energy.

Temporary service location with active highlighting can receive more visibility in relevant local service discovery.

This keeps the tool useful but reduces abuse.

---

## Highlighted temporary location

If a service provider has active service highlighting, temporary service locations can receive enhanced visibility.

Example:

User searches tow truck in Tallinn.

A highlighted tow truck provider has active temporary location in Tallinn until 16:00.

It may appear in:

- relevant service results
- featured nearby service area
- profile quick update
- followers' updates

This creates real value for the provider and the user.

---

## Abuse prevention for temporary locations

Temporary locations can be abused if a provider marks many places where they are not actually available.

Basic controls:

- one free temporary service location per day
- additional temporary locations require Energy
- each update expires automatically
- all updates are logged
- misleading updates can be reported
- repeated abuse can reduce trust or visibility
- admin review can be triggered for suspicious patterns

Do not build full live GPS tracking for Launch.

Architecture should support stronger validation later if needed.

---

## Trust effect of updates

Quick updates and temporary service locations are visible to followers and relevant users.

If a provider repeatedly posts unreliable availability, users may unfollow or stop using that provider.

This creates natural trust pressure.

However, Selqiro should not rely only on social pressure.

The platform must keep update history and allow reports and moderation.

Trust is a business asset.

Providers who use quick updates honestly should gain value.

Providers who misuse them should lose visibility over time.


---

## Updated temporary service location pricing decision

Temporary service location Energy model should be duration-based, not per extra location only.

This supersedes earlier simple wording about charging mainly by number of additional locations.

Launch principle:

- one free temporary service location update per day
- paid temporary location access can be purchased for a time period
- 1 day has the highest cost per day
- 14 days has a lower cost per day
- 30 days has the best cost per day

Reason:

Some service providers use location updates as a real work tool.

Examples:
- tow truck
- mobile repair service
- plumber
- taxi-like service later
- mobile equipment service

For these providers, longer access should be more cost-effective.

This encourages honest professional use instead of constant short-term micro-purchases.

---

## One active temporary location at a time

Paid temporary location access should not mean that a provider can appear in many cities at the same time.

Recommended rule:

A service provider may have one active temporary service location at a time per service.

When they update the location, the previous active temporary location is replaced.

Example:

12:00 Tallinn, Gonsiori street
↓
15:00 Tartu, Ringtee
↓
19:00 Paide

The provider is not simultaneously visible in all three places.

This protects trust and prevents abuse.

---

## Temporary location period options

Possible period options:

- free: one temporary location update per day
- 1 day access
- 14 day access
- 30 day access

The 30 day option should be the most cost-effective per day.

This supports serious service providers who use Selqiro as a daily work tool.

---

## Temporary location and highlighting

Temporary location access and service highlighting are related but separate.

Temporary location:
- tells Selqiro where the service is currently available

Service highlighting:
- increases discovery visibility

If a provider has both:
- active temporary service location
- active service highlighting

then the service may receive stronger visibility in the relevant local area and category.

Example:

A tow truck has active temporary location in Tallinn until 16:00 and active highlighting.

User searches tow truck near Tallinn.

The provider can appear in featured nearby tow truck services.

---

## Abuse prevention

Temporary location access should remain trust-based but controlled.

Controls:

- one active temporary location at a time
- location updates are logged
- updates expire or are replaced
- misleading location updates can be reported
- repeated misuse can reduce trust or visibility
- admin review can be triggered by suspicious patterns

Do not build full live GPS tracking for launch.

Architecture should allow stronger validation later if needed.
