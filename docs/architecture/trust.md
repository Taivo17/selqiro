# Trust Architecture

## Purpose

Trust Architecture defines how Selqiro builds confidence between users, sellers, service providers, businesses and the platform.

Trust is one of Selqiro's most important long-term assets.

Users should feel:

I can safely browse, contact, buy, sell and use services here.

Businesses should feel:

Selqiro treats me fairly and helps me build credibility.

---

## Core principle

Trust should be built quietly.

Selqiro should not overload users with scores, warnings and badges.

Trust signals should help users make better decisions without making the platform feel suspicious or stressful.

---

## Trust is not only reviews

Reviews can be useful later, but they should not be the first trust system.

Early marketplaces do not have enough real data for reliable reviews.

Launch should begin with quiet trust signals.

Examples:

- identity created date
- active recently
- verified email
- complete profile
- verified business later
- report-free status
- response behavior later
- admin verified later

---

## Trust layers

Selqiro trust should have several layers:

1. Account trust
2. Identity trust
3. Business trust
4. Listing trust
5. Service trust
6. Message trust
7. Discovery trust
8. Future Knowledge trust

Each layer has different signals.

---

## Account trust

Account trust is private platform-level trust.

Possible account signals:

- email verified
- login history
- suspicious activity
- account age
- payment behavior later
- abuse reports
- multiple identity behavior
- device or fraud signals later

Account trust should mostly be internal.

Public users usually do not need to see account-level data.

---

## Identity trust

Identity trust is public-facing.

Possible identity signals:

- identity age
- complete profile
- avatar/logo
- location
- active recently
- number of listings or services
- no serious reports
- admin reviewed later

Identity trust helps users understand who they are interacting with.

---

## Business trust

Business trust may include:

- business profile completeness
- registration number later
- VAT number later
- verified business later
- official website later
- business location
- active services
- response behavior
- history on Selqiro
- admin verification later

Business verification should be introduced carefully.

Do not promise verification before the process is reliable.

---

## Listing trust

Listing trust may include:

- clear title
- real images
- price consistency
- category match
- location
- seller identity
- image quality
- duplicate detection later
- suspicious price detection later
- report signals

AI can help detect suspicious listings.

AI should flag risk, not automatically punish in uncertain cases.

---

## Service trust

Service trust may include:

- complete service description
- business identity
- location or service area
- contact availability
- active recently
- verified business later
- no serious reports
- service category relevance

Service trust is especially important because users may invite a provider into real life work.

---

## Message trust

Messages must respect identity and privacy.

Messaging trust includes:

- clear partner identity
- related listing/service context
- block support
- report support
- unread indicators
- no self-contact confusion
- no broken conversation state after identity switch

Private messages should not be casually inspected.

Admin access should be case-based.

---

## Discovery trust

Discovery is high visibility.

Trust rules for discovery are stricter.

Today's Story and featured discovery must be:

- reviewed
- clearly marked
- relevant
- non-political
- non-divisive
- connected to Selqiro content
- not misleading

Paid discovery must not override organic ranking secretly.

---

## Knowledge trust later

Knowledge Gateway requires the strongest trust model.

Future Knowledge answers may involve technical or professional information.

Rules:

- AI must not pretend certainty
- sources should be indicated when possible
- confidence should be shown
- official or professional sources should be preferred
- if confidence is low, say so
- correctness is more important than speed
- trust before cost

Knowledge trust is a future major module.

Launch does not require full Knowledge Gateway.

---

## Trust signals in UI

Trust signals should be subtle.

Examples:

- Verified business
- Active recently
- Complete profile
- New seller
- Identity since 2026
- Responds usually within 1 day later

Avoid overwhelming users with too many badges.

One or two useful signals are better than ten noisy badges.

---

## Verified business

Verified business should be added only when the verification process is real.

Possible verification inputs:

- registration number
- VAT number
- official email domain
- website
- manual admin review
- payment/billing consistency
- future external registry checks

Do not fake verification.

If Selqiro says verified, it must mean something.

---

## Reputation

Public reputation should come later.

Do not launch with an empty star rating system.

Ratings and reviews can be manipulated if added too early.

Future reputation may include:

- completed interactions
- response quality
- verified business
- dispute history
- customer feedback
- moderation signals
- trust score

But launch should use quiet signals first.

---

## Reports

Reports are trust signals.

But reports are not automatic proof.

Reports should be reviewed.

False or malicious reports should not harm honest users automatically.

Report count should not be public by default.

Admin should use reports as part of the trust picture.

---

## Blocks

Blocks protect users.

Block behavior should be strong.

If a user blocks another account, that blocked account should not easily bypass the block through another identity.

Block is stronger than follow.

Blocking should affect:

- messaging
- marketplace visibility where appropriate
- feed/discovery visibility where appropriate

---

## Fraud and abuse prevention

Trust system should support abuse prevention.

Risks:

- fake accounts
- fake listings
- fake locations
- spam services
- fake business identities
- Energy abuse
- fake invitations
- fake Country Pioneer claims
- stolen images
- duplicate content
- scam messages

Launch does not need full fraud engine.

Architecture should support adding one later.

---

## Trust and Energy

Energy systems can be abused.

Examples:

- fake invited users
- fake pioneer claims
- refund abuse
- stolen payment methods
- bot AI usage
- mass account creation

Energy-related trust checks should exist before large-scale Energy rewards.

Recognition Energy must be rare and meaningful.

---

## Trust and payments

Payment behavior can influence internal trust.

Examples:

- successful business payments
- failed payments
- chargebacks
- refunds
- suspicious payment patterns

This should be internal.

Do not show payment trust publicly unless it becomes part of verified business later.

---

## Trust and admin

Admin tools should show trust context.

Example admin identity view:

- identity age
- owner account
- reports
- blocks
- active listings
- services
- verification status
- Energy/payment concerns later
- moderation history
- admin notes

Admin should have enough context to make fair decisions.

---

## Trust and privacy

Trust must not violate privacy.

Do not expose private account data just to create trust.

Examples:

Do not show:
- private email
- exact login history
- private payment info
- private messages without report context

Do show:
- public identity
- verified business status
- active profile
- basic public signals

Privacy is part of trust.

---

## Launch trust scope

V2 Launch should include:

- clear public identity
- report
- block
- admin moderation basics
- complete profile indicators
- active identity visibility
- no self-contact confusion
- basic business profile completeness
- safe messaging context

Launch does not need:

- full reviews
- public star ratings
- full fraud engine
- advanced reputation score
- external registry verification
- public trust score

---

## Future trust modules

Future trust work may include:

- verified business
- identity age display
- response time
- reputation
- reviews
- dispute resolution
- fraud scoring
- registry checks
- seller verification
- trust analytics
- Knowledge source confidence

---

## What trust should feel like

Trust should feel:

- calm
- fair
- transparent
- privacy-respecting
- not suspicious by default

User should think:

I understand who I am dealing with.

Business should think:

Selqiro helps me look credible if I act honestly.

---

## Final rule

Trust is not a badge.

Trust is the result of consistent, fair and transparent platform behavior.

---

## Public visibility metrics

Exact public view counts should not be shown during launch.

Reason:

- low early numbers can make good listings feel weak
- users may overvalue popularity instead of relevance
- public counters can create unnecessary pressure
- marketplace should first build trust and usefulness

Listing owners may see detailed performance analytics.

Public visitors should see a clean listing page.

Future soft signals may be added after real usage grows.

Examples:

- Popular
- Getting attention today
- Recently viewed by many users

Exact metrics remain owner/admin analytics unless there is a strong product reason to expose them later.

---

## Trust and quick updates

Quick updates and temporary service locations affect trust.

If a provider posts unreliable availability, users may lose trust.

Examples of misuse:

- posting temporary location where provider is not available
- repeatedly changing locations only to appear in more searches
- marking urgent availability without real service capacity

Trust protections:

- update history
- automatic expiration
- user reports
- admin review
- repeated misuse can reduce visibility later
- future trust score can consider reliability of updates

Honest quick updates should help providers.

Misleading quick updates should hurt long-term trust.
