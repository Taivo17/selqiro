# Jobs Flow

## Purpose

Jobs Flow describes how businesses can publish job opportunities in Selqiro.

Jobs are future-facing but should be supported by architecture.

Selqiro should start with employer-side job postings.

That means:

A company is looking for a worker.

Not:

A person is posting "I am looking for work" as a marketplace listing.

This keeps the job system simple and clear.

---

## Core principle

Jobs should be simple opportunities.

A job posting should help a person quickly understand:

- what the role is
- who is hiring
- where the work is
- whether salary is shown
- how to apply or contact

Do not make the first version a full recruitment platform.

---

## Employer-side jobs

Initial Jobs model:

Business identity creates a job posting.

Example:

Paide Puksiirabi OÜ is looking for:
Tow truck driver

The job belongs to the business identity and appears:

- in the business profile / Brand Space
- in future Jobs section
- in relevant nearby job discovery
- in future job alerts

---

## What is not included initially

Initial Jobs should not include:

- personal "looking for work" listings
- full CV database
- recruiter marketplace
- complex application tracking
- interviews
- contracts
- payroll
- employment verification
- candidate ranking AI

These can be considered later if real demand exists.

---

## Job posting fields

A simple job posting should support:

Required or strongly recommended:

- job title
- business identity
- location
- short description
- contact or apply action

Optional:

- salary
- salary range
- employment type
- working time
- experience requirement
- start date
- remote/hybrid/on-site
- application deadline
- image or company logo

If an optional field is empty, it should not be shown publicly.

Rule:

Show what exists.
Hide what is empty.

---

## Salary behavior

Salary should be optional.

Some companies may want to show salary.

Some may not.

Allowed display examples:

- 1200–1600 € / month
- From 15 € / hour
- Salary by agreement
- no salary shown

If salary is not added, do not show an empty salary area.

---

## Location behavior

Location is important for jobs.

A job should support:

- city
- country
- address if useful
- remote / hybrid / on-site option later

Jobs can later connect to "near me" discovery.

Example:

Notify me when a mechanic job appears within 30 km.

---

## Job card

Job card should be simple.

It can show:

- job title
- company name
- location
- salary if available
- employment type if available
- freshness
- save action later

Example:

Tow truck driver
Paide Puksiirabi OÜ
Paide
Full-time

---

## Job detail

Job detail should show:

- job title
- company name
- location
- description
- requirements if added
- salary if added
- employment type if added
- contact or apply action
- link to Brand Space

The job detail should not feel like a complex HR system.

It is a simple opportunity page.

---

## Apply / contact action

Recommended action labels:

- Kandideeri
- Võta ühendust
- Kirjuta

If Selqiro does not support full application flow yet, use "Võta ühendust" or "Kirjuta".

Future:

"Kandideeri" can open a simple application flow when supported.

---

## Brand Space connection

Jobs should be a module inside Brand Space.

Business profile may show:

- active jobs
- latest job openings
- "View all jobs"

If no jobs exist, do not show empty jobs module publicly.

Owner view can suggest:

Add your first job posting.

---

## My Area connection

Jobs should be managed inside My Area for identities with job module enabled.

My Area / Jobs can support:

- add job
- edit job
- pause job
- close job
- archive job
- view applicants later
- messages related to job

Launch does not require full applicant management.

---

## Discovery

Future discovery can show:

- jobs near me
- jobs from followed businesses
- featured jobs
- jobs in my saved categories
- new jobs alerts

Paid visibility may exist later but must be clearly marked.

Organic job discovery must remain trustworthy.

---

## Notifications

Future feature:

Notify me when a job appears near me.

Example:

User wants mechanic jobs within 30 km.

Selqiro notifies when matching job appears.

This can be valuable for job seekers without building a full job platform at launch.

---

## AI support

AI can help businesses create better job postings.

Examples:

- improve job title
- make description clearer
- suggest missing fields
- translate job posting
- simplify requirements

AI should not invent salary, benefits or requirements.

User remains in control.

---

## Moderation

Jobs should be moderated for:

- misleading claims
- discrimination
- fake jobs
- unsafe work
- illegal work
- scam-like postings
- political or ideological recruitment disguised as job

Launch moderation can be simple.

Architecture should support review later.

---

## What Jobs should feel like

For businesses:

I can quickly say we are hiring.

For users:

I can quickly see what jobs are available near me.

For Selqiro:

Jobs are another opportunity type, not a separate complicated platform.

---

## Final rule

Jobs in Selqiro start as simple employer-side opportunities.

A business is looking for a worker.

The system should stay simple and grow only if real usage shows the need.
